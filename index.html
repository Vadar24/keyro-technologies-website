For emergency-service trades

Your office closes.
Your front office doesn’t.

For plumbing, HVAC, restoration, electrical, and similar businesses, VEYRA can identify urgent inbound requests, collect structured information, and route or escalate them according to your predefined business protocols — around the clock.

VEYRA’s role is intake, qualification, communication, scheduling, routing, and escalation. VEYRA does not provide professional plumbing, electrical, medical, emergency, or safety advice.
